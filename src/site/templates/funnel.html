Committee funnel

Bills assigned to each committee and the fraction that became Republic Acts. Per chamber, per Congress, with a combined view. Ranked committees appear first; committees below the ranking floor are collapsed at the bottom of each section.

Reading this table

Touch rate, not exit rate. A bill assigned to N committees counts once in each. A 40% rate means 40% of the bills assigned to this committee eventually became RAs — not that this committee shepherded them there.

Ranking floor: 10 assigned bills. Committees with fewer than 10 assigned bills are collapsed below each ranked table as "below the floor — unranked." A 2-bill, 2-RA committee is not a 100% funnel; it is noise.

Special committees can lead at modest n. A special committee that handles 15 bills and produces 12 RAs will outrank a regular committee with 800 bills and a 40% rate. The floor exists to prevent absurd results, not to enforce chamber-hierarchy realism.

Data source

Every row is traceable to data/analytics/committee_funnel.csv, derived from data/curated/committees.parquet and data/curated/bills.parquet. The rate column is ras / assigned_bills.

Four House committee codes remain unmapped and appear below as code-as-name: J506 (32 rows), K503, K504, K505 (1 row each, all in the 19th Congress). See the repository's README limitations section for detail.
