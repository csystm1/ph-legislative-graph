Philippine Legislative Bills Graph

Co-authorship and committee-funnel analysis of Philippine Senate and House bills filed during the 17th, 18th, and 19th Congresses (2016–2025). Built from bettergovph/open-congress-data.

What this is

Three analyses over the metadata of __TOTAL_BILLS__ filed bills (__TOTAL_SENATE__ Senate, __TOTAL_HOUSE__ House):

  • Co-authorship network — who files with whom, community structure within each chamber-Congress.
  • Committee funnel — bills assigned to each committee and the fraction that became Republic Acts.
  • Temporal evolution — how the graph and funnel change across the 17th, 18th, and 19th Congresses.

The unit of analysis is the bill, not the vote: which legislative relationships the filing system actually encodes.

Key findings

  • House graphs are dense but weakly modular. Each 17th–19th House graph spans __HOUSE_NODES__ legislators and __HOUSE_EDGES__ co-authorship edges, with Louvain modularity between __HOUSE_MOD_MIN__ and __HOUSE_MOD_MAX__. The signal is broad overlap between blocs, not clean partition into them. source: data/analytics/graph_stats.csv
  • Senate co-authorship is near-universal. Each Senate Congress spans just 24 legislators, and the co-authorship graph is essentially complete: __S17_EDGES__ of 276 possible pairs in the 17th, 276 of 276 in the 18th, 276 of 276 in the 19th. Every pair of senators co-authored at least one bill in the 18th and 19th Congresses; isolation is negligible: __S17_ISOLATED__ in the 17th, and zero in both the 18th and 19th. Bulk coauthorship motions ("all Senators present be made coauthors") drive the density. source: data/analytics/graph_stats.csv
  • House graphs carry the only meaningful community signal. Senate co-authorship graphs are complete or nearly complete cliques (268–276 of 276 possible pairs per Congress), so Louvain returns modularity ≈ 0 by definition. The interpretable community structure in this corpus lives on the House side — 3–4 nontrivial communities per Congress at modularity 0.054–0.097, reflecting real bloc overlap in a 301–316-member chamber. Senate modularity values should not be read as evidence of absence of coalition structure. source: data/analytics/graph_stats.csv
  • Committee funnel rankings favor the committees that carry enacted bills. House-17 Rules touches __H17_RULES_RATE__ of its __H17_RULES_N__ assigned bills that became RAs; Appropriations touches __H17_APPS_RATE__ of __H17_APPS_N__. The funnel records where bills pass through, not where they die. source: data/analytics/committee_funnel.csv
  • Communities are not cross-Congress comparable. Each graph is clustered independently. A community id in house-17 is not the same coalition as the same id in house-19. Temporal comparisons read node/edge/community counts, not community identity. source: data/analytics/communities/*.parquet

Data and method

Source: bettergovph/open-congress-data, a community-maintained TOML corpus of Philippine legislative documents. Scope is the 17th–19th Congress (2016–2025); the 20th Congress is excluded as incomplete upstream.

Graph edges are co-authorship only. The primary sponsor of a bill is recorded as a node attribute, not as an edge — a distinction that keeps Louvain from clustering by filing volume. See the repository's Analyses section for the full rationale and known limitations.
